What is Pluck

  1. 1 Pluck is a small menu-bar app for Mac. It lives up in the menu bar — no window, no dock icon clutter.
  2. 2 Paste a YouTube link and Pluck downloads the audio and saves it as an .m4a file in your Downloads folder.
  3. 3 Everything runs on your Mac. There are no accounts and no sign-in.
💡 Pluck pairs well with Song Buddy — grab a track with Pluck, then open the .m4a in Song Buddy to split it into stems and practise along.
📦

Install

  1. 1 Download Pluck.zip and double-click it to unzip. You'll get Pluck.app.
  2. 2 Drag Pluck.app into your Applications folder.
  3. 3 The first time you open it, right-click the app and choose Open, then confirm. macOS asks this once for apps downloaded outside the App Store.
  4. 4 Pluck appears as a ☘️ shamrock in your menu bar. Click it to open the little download panel.
💡 If macOS still blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, and click Open Anyway.
🛠️

Requirements

  1. 1 Pluck uses two free command-line tools to do the actual downloading and conversion: yt-dlp and ffmpeg. The easiest way to get them is Homebrew.
  2. 2 If you don't have Homebrew yet, install it by pasting this into Terminal: $ /bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Homebrew/install/HEAD/install.sh)"
  3. 3 Then install both tools with one command: $ brew install yt-dlp ffmpeg
  4. 4 That's it — Pluck finds them automatically. To keep downloads working as YouTube changes, update yt-dlp now and then: $ brew upgrade yt-dlp
▶️

Downloading a Song

  1. 1 Copy a YouTube link from your browser, then click the ☘️ in the menu bar. If the link is on your clipboard, Pluck pastes it in for you automatically.
  2. 2 If it wasn't auto-filled, paste the link into the Paste YouTube URL… field. Pluck accepts youtube.com, youtu.be, and music.youtube.com links.
  3. 3 Click Download (or press Return). A progress bar shows the download, then a short Converting… step while it makes the .m4a.
  4. 4 Need to stop? Click Cancel at any time.
  5. 5 When it's done, click Show in Finder to jump straight to the file, or Clear to reset for the next one.
📁

Where Files Go

  1. 1 Every download lands in your Downloads folder, named after the video title, as an .m4a audio file.
  2. 2 .m4a plays in Music, QuickTime, and just about any app — and opens directly in Song Buddy for stem separation.
🩺

Troubleshooting

  1. 1 "yt-dlp not found" or "ffmpeg not found" — the tools aren't installed. Run brew install yt-dlp ffmpeg (see Requirements above), then try again.
  2. 2 A download fails or errors out — YouTube changes often, which can break the downloader. Update it with brew upgrade yt-dlp and retry.
  3. 3 "Not a YouTube URL" — Pluck only handles YouTube links. Make sure the address starts with youtube.com, youtu.be, or music.youtube.com.
  4. 4 macOS won't open the app — right-click Pluck.app and choose Open, or allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security.
💡 Please only download content you have the right to use. Pluck is a tool for grabbing your own uploads, public-domain material, and anything you're permitted to save.
